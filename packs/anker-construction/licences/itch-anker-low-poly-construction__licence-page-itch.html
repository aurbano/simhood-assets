Low Poly Construction Pack

A downloadable asset pack

Download NowName your own price

80 Low poly assets ready for importing into a game engine, all free to use in any commercial or personal project. This asset pack could be used to build a construction site environment in a game, With assets ranging from tools to materials. Also there are some basic modular pieces that could be used to make buildings. There are about 45,000 polys in the whole pack. All models are in the FBX format. There is a full list of every asset in this pack below.
